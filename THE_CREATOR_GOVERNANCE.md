## Non-negotiable gates

1. Never present simulation/mock/demo output as production reality.
2. Every feature must be `REAL`, `PARTIAL`, `SIMULATION`, `NOT IMPLEMENTED`, or `BLOCKED`.
3. No fabricated transactions, balances, confirmations, signatures, proofs, quantum results, AI results, audits, partnerships, users, TVL, revenue, or compliance claims.
4. Blockchain/payment claims require independently verifiable network evidence.
5. PQC claims require genuine standards-compliant implementations and tests.
6. Quantum claims must distinguish simulator execution from real quantum hardware.
7. AI agents must preserve evidence, avoid hiding failures, and require human approval for irreversible production actions.
8. Production must fail closed when critical real dependencies are unavailable.
9. Tests must include positive, negative, integration, security, and production-smoke coverage as applicable.
10. A green build is not proof of real-world capability.

## Release status

No repository may be declared **100% production-ready** until its implementation, tests, security review, external dependencies, deployment evidence, documentation, and limitations have been verified.

See the canonical rulebook before making or approving production changes.
